## The short answer

Xiaomian is Chongqing's everyday noodle bowl: wheat noodles, chili oil, peanuts, pickles, scallions and a savory sauce. It is cheap, fast and usually eaten for breakfast.

A good shop has a short menu, a steady stream of locals and a cook who is moving quickly. You do not need a famous address. You need the right hour and a clean, busy counter.

## What is in the bowl

The base is usually a mix of chili oil, soy sauce, vinegar, garlic water, sesame paste and ground peanuts. Pickles add salt and crunch. Some shops add minced pork, braised beef or a soft-boiled egg.

The noodles are typically thin wheat noodles. They are boiled briefly and tossed with the sauce. "Dry" means little broth. "Soup" means more liquid.

## How to order

Useful words:

- **小面 (xiǎo miàn):** the basic noodle bowl.
- **牛肉面 (niú ròu miàn):** beef noodle.
- **杂酱面 (zá jiàng miàn):** noodles with minced meat sauce.
- **干溜 (gān liū):** dry style, less broth.
- **汤面 (tāng miàn):** soup style.
- **微辣 (wēi là):** mild.
- **不要辣 (bú yào là):** no chili.

Point at the menu and hold up the number of fingers. Most shops understand the request.

## How to judge a shop

- **A short menu.** A shop that does a few things well is usually better than one with fifty options.
- **A busy counter.** Locals eating quickly is a good sign.
- **Fresh noodles.** Look for a cook working through a pile of orders.
- **Clean bowls.** Speed should not come at the cost of hygiene.
- **A mix of ages.** A breakfast shop used by workers, students and older residents is part of the neighborhood.

## How to eat it

Mix the noodles from the bottom so the sauce coats every strand. Taste before adding more chili. If the bowl is too hot, add a little vinegar or order a soy milk.

The peanuts and pickles are part of the texture. Do not pick them out on the first visit.

## Where to look

Good xiaomian is distributed across the city rather than concentrated in one food street. Look near metro exits, residential compounds, schools and morning markets. Jiefangbei has options, but the best value is often one block away from the main pedestrian streets.

In the morning, follow the people carrying bowls or standing at a counter. In the evening, some shops switch to noodles as a late snack.

## What it costs

A basic bowl is usually CNY 7–12. Beef or extra toppings push it to CNY 15–25. It is one of the best-value meals in the city.

## The perfect first order

Order a basic xiaomian with mild chili and a soy milk. Mix well, taste, then add a little more chili if you want. Eat it at the counter or on a stool. That is the full Chongqing breakfast experience.
