## The short answer

This three-day plan groups stops by district and elevation. Day one covers the Jialing River side and the night skyline. Day two crosses the Yangtze and explores the old streets. Day three opens up for Ciqikou, a museum or a day trip.

Stay near Jiefangbei. Start outdoor sightseeing early, keep the middle of the day flexible and save one evening for an unscheduled meal.

## Day 1: Jialing River and the skyline

### Morning

Arrive and check in. If your room is not ready, leave your bags and walk to a nearby noodle shop for breakfast. Do not plan a major sight on arrival day.

### 15:00 — Liziba

Take Metro Line 2 to Liziba. Watch the train pass through the building from the street-level platform, then ride one stop to see the track from inside.

Walk downhill toward the river for a wider view. The path takes 20–30 minutes and includes stairs.

### 17:00 — Eling Park

Take a short taxi or metro ride to Eling Park. Walk the ridge for a view over the Jialing River and the city layers. This is a good place to slow down before dinner.

### 18:30 — Dinner near Jiefangbei

Choose a casual restaurant or a hotpot place. If you plan hotpot, keep the order small and save room for the next day.

### 20:00 — Hongyadong

Walk to Qiansimen Bridge for the classic night view. Arrive before the crowd peaks, take your photos and leave the interior for another visit.

## Day 2: Yangtze crossing and old streets

### 07:30 — Breakfast

Find a xiaomian shop near the hotel. Order a basic bowl and a soy milk.

### 08:30 — Yangtze River Cableway

Take a taxi or metro to the Yuzhong station. Buy a one-way ticket to Nan'an before the queue builds. Ride across and walk downhill to the riverfront.

### 10:00 — Nanbin Road

Walk a section of the promenade. The skyline is clearest in the morning, and the river breeze makes the walk comfortable.

### 11:30 — Longmenhao

Take a taxi to Longmenhao. Walk the stairs, look at the old houses and stop for tea. This is a quieter alternative to Ciqikou.

### 13:00 — Lunch in Nan'an

Choose a local restaurant away from the main road. Order one cold dish, one vegetable and one meat dish to share.

### 15:00 — Shibati or Kuixinglou

Cross back to Yuzhong and walk one of the stair neighborhoods. Pick one, not both, if the heat is strong.

### 18:30 — Hotpot

Book or arrive early at a hotpot restaurant. Order a split pot, beef, tofu skin, potato and greens. Take your time.

### 21:00 — River walk

Walk to Chaotianmen or the riverside near Jiefangbei. The city is at its best after the lights come on.

## Day 3: Choose your direction

### Option A: Ciqikou and the west

Go to Ciqikou by 08:30. Walk the main street, then turn uphill into the side lanes. Have a snack and leave before the tour groups arrive.

In the afternoon, visit Testbed 2 or a museum. Finish with a casual dinner in Shapingba.

### Option B: Wulong day trip

Take an early high-speed train to Wulong. Visit the karst scenery, then return in the evening. This is a long day and needs advance planning.

### Option C: Slow city day

Visit the Three Gorges Museum, walk the人民广场 area and take a tea break. Add a river cruise in the evening.

## Where to stay

Jiefangbei is the best base for this plan. It puts the first and second nights within walking distance of the river and the metro. If you want a view, choose a high floor in Nan'an for the last night.

## What to book

- Hotel for three nights.
- Cableway ticket if the queue is a concern.
- Any day trip train tickets.
- Hotpot reservation for a weekend evening.
- Airport transfer or metro plan.

## The rule for a good day

Do one major view in the morning, one neighborhood in the afternoon and one food experience at night. Chongqing rewards that rhythm more than a long list of pins.
