## The short answer

The Dazu Rock Carvings are a UNESCO World Heritage site of Buddhist, Taoist and Confucian figures carved into sandstone cliffs. The main visit is Baodingshan, about 1.5 hours from central Chongqing by train and taxi.

It is an easy half-day or full-day escape from the city, with far fewer crowds than the central attractions.

## Why go

The carvings date from the 9th to 13th centuries and cover a remarkable range of subjects: deities, guardians, scenes of daily life and moral lessons. The Baodingshan site is compact and deeply textured, with figures arranged in niches and around a central courtyard.

The setting is also pleasant. Trees, stone walls and temple roofs create a quieter atmosphere than the city.

## The route

1. Take a high-speed train from Chongqing West to Dazu South. The ride is about 40–50 minutes.
2. From Dazu South, take a taxi or local bus to Baodingshan. The transfer takes 20–30 minutes.
3. Buy the ticket and walk the main circuit.
4. Spend two to three hours at the site.
5. Return to the station with a buffer for the train.

Check the train times before you go. The station is outside the town, and the transfer can take longer during rain or holidays.

## What to see

- **Baodingshan:** the main carving site and the best introduction.
- **The reclining Buddha:** a large, serene figure set into the cliff.
- **The Thousand-Hand Guanyin:** a dense, gilded composition.
- **The temple courtyard:** a quiet place to sit between the rock walls.

If you have more time, other Dazu sites such as Beishan are worth a longer stay. For a day trip, Baodingshan is enough.

## Practical notes

- Wear shoes with grip.
- Carry water and sun protection.
- Check whether photography is allowed in each area.
- Do not touch the carvings.
- Keep your voice low in the temple spaces.

## Cost

Train tickets vary by class and departure. Add the taxi or bus, the entrance ticket and lunch. A comfortable day trip is usually cheaper than a guided tour and gives you more control over the pace.

## The pace

Dazu rewards slow looking. Do not rush from niche to niche. Sit in the courtyard, watch the light change and let the details appear.

## Combine it with

Return to Chongqing in the late afternoon and have a relaxed dinner near Jiefangbei. The contrast between the quiet carvings and the noisy city is part of the trip.
