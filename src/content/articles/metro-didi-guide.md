## The short answer

Use the metro for river crossings and long distances. Use Didi or a taxi for short, steep connections and late nights. Set up the metro QR code in Alipay before your first ride.

The main challenge is the exit, not the train. Exit numbers can surface several streets and many stairs apart.

## Metro basics

Chongqing's metro is clean, frequent and cheap. Fares usually range from CNY 2 to CNY 10 depending on distance. Signs and announcements are in Chinese and English.

Key lines for visitors:

- **Line 1:** Jiefangbei, Ciqikou and the western city.
- **Line 2:** Liziba, Eling and the Jialing River corridor.
- **Line 3:** Airport, Guanyinqiao and the Liangjiang area.
- **Line 6:** River crossings and connections to the peninsula.
- **Line 10:** Airport and the northern districts.

## Paying

Open Alipay, find the transport or metro QR code and scan it at the gate. The fare is deducted automatically. If the QR code fails, buy a single ticket at a machine.

Keep your phone charged. A dead phone means a ticket queue.

## Choosing the exit

Before you tap out, check the exit letter and the street name. Use the station map and a map app. If you are meeting a driver, choose a landmark above ground rather than an underground exit.

Some stations have long transfer tunnels. Allow extra time when changing lines with luggage.

## Didi

Didi is the local ride-hailing app. The English interface lets you set a pickup point, destination and payment method. The app shows the plate number and route.

Tips:

- Set the pickup point at a recognizable entrance.
- Message the driver with a landmark if the pin is unclear.
- Check the plate before getting in.
- Keep the destination in Chinese.

## Taxis

Official taxis use meters. Show the address in Chinese and confirm the meter is running. Avoid drivers who approach you inside stations or airports.

Payment is usually by QR code. Ask **kě yǐ sǎo mǎ ma** if you want to scan. Keep small cash as backup.

## Rush hour

Avoid the metro between 07:30–09:30 and 17:30–19:30 if you have luggage. The trains are frequent, but the platforms are crowded.

Rain slows road traffic dramatically. Add 20–30 minutes to taxi journeys.

## Safety

- Keep bags in front of you on crowded trains.
- Do not block escalator exits.
- Hold the handrail on steep station stairs.
- Check the last train time before a late dinner.

## The simple rule

If the destination is across a river or more than 3 km away, take the metro. If it is uphill, raining or late, take a taxi. That combination handles most of Chongqing.
