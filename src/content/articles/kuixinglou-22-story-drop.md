## The short answer

Kuixinglou is a residential tower where one street meets the building at the top and another meets it far below. The famous "22-story drop" is the view from the upper road into the lower courtyard.

It is free, quick and close to Jiefangbei. Treat it as a ten-minute urban observation rather than a full attraction.

## Why it looks impossible

Chongqing's streets follow the contour of the land. A building can have a proper entrance on a upper road and another entrance at the bottom of the hill. The tower itself is ordinary. The geography makes it extraordinary.

The best-known view looks down from the upper road toward the lower buildings. The height difference reads as a cliff, even though the structure is a normal residential block.

## Finding the viewpoint

Search for the Chinese name **魁星楼** in a map app. The exact approach can change with construction and footpath closures. From Jiefangbei, take a short taxi or walk uphill through the Yuzhong lanes.

Look for the point where the road bends around the building and the lower courtyard opens below. If you reach a residential entrance with residents coming and going, you are close.

## Etiquette

This is a lived-in building, not a viewing platform.

- Keep your voice low.
- Do not block doorways or staircases.
- Ask before photographing residents.
- Do not fly a drone over the courtyard.
- Move on after a few minutes.

The best way to keep access open is to behave like a guest in a neighborhood.

## The photo

Stand at the road edge and include the building's upper entrance in the frame. A person walking into the lower courtyard gives the image scale. A wide lens exaggerates the drop. A vertical frame shows the road, the tower and the lower street in one composition.

Avoid leaning over railings or stepping into traffic. The view is dramatic enough without taking risks.

## Combine it with

- **Jiefangbei:** 10–20 minutes away on foot or by taxi.
- **Shibati:** a nearby cluster of stair streets and reconstructed lanes.
- **Hongyadong:** continue downhill for the evening view.
- **The Yangtze cableway:** a short ride toward the river.

## Is it worth it?

Yes, if you are already in Yuzhong and want to understand how the city fits together. It is not worth a long detour on its own. Pair it with Shibati or Hongyadong and let it be one small, memorable piece of the day.
