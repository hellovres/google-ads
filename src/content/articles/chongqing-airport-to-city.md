## The short answer

Take Metro Line 10 from Jiangbei Airport T3 for the cheapest trip. Transfer to Line 6 or another line for Jiefangbei. Use a taxi or Didi if you have large bags, arrive late or want a direct ride.

Allow 50–70 minutes by metro and 35–55 minutes by road, depending on traffic.

## Metro

Line 10 connects the airport to the city. The station is inside the terminal complex, with signs in English. Buy a ticket at a machine or use the metro QR code in Alipay.

For Jiefangbei, ride Line 10 to Hongtudi and transfer to Line 6 toward Xiaoshizi. The walk from Xiaoshizi to the Jiefangbei hotels is short but includes stairs and crowded sidewalks.

The metro is cheap and reliable. It is less convenient with a large suitcase during rush hour.

## Taxi

The official taxi rank is outside the arrivals hall. Follow the signs and join the queue. Do not accept offers from drivers who approach you inside the terminal.

Show the driver the hotel name and address in Chinese. The ride to Jiefangbei usually costs CNY 60–100. Traffic can add time, especially in the evening.

Keep the meter running. If the driver suggests a fixed price, confirm it is reasonable or ask to use the meter.

## Didi

Didi works with an English interface. Set the pickup point carefully, because the airport has multiple levels and exits. The app will show the car and plate number.

Didi is useful when the taxi queue is long or when your hotel is difficult to explain. The price is usually similar to a taxi.

## Late arrivals

Metro service ends at night. Check the last train time for your arrival date. If you land late, book a hotel transfer or use the official taxi rank.

Confirm that your hotel has overnight reception. Some smaller properties close the front desk.

## With luggage

- Use the elevator, not the escalator, with a large case.
- Keep bags close on the metro.
- Allow extra time for transfers.
- A taxi is easier for two or more people with bags.

## The best choice

For a daytime arrival with light luggage, take the metro. For a late arrival, a group or a heavy bag, take a taxi or Didi. The difference in cost is small compared with the stress of a complicated transfer.
