## The short answer

Many Chongqing attractions sell timed tickets through WeChat mini programs. Search the Chinese name of the attraction inside WeChat, open the official mini program and book with your passport details.

If the booking flow is confusing, use the official ticket counter, your hotel desk or a reputable booking platform.

## What a mini program is

A mini program is an app inside WeChat. It opens without a separate download. Attractions, museums, cableways and transport services use them for reservations, queue numbers and digital tickets.

The interface is usually in Chinese. A translation app helps, but the key fields are predictable: date, time slot, visitor count, name and ID number.

## The booking flow

1. Open WeChat and search the attraction's Chinese name.
2. Choose the official account or mini program with a verified badge.
3. Select the date and time slot.
4. Enter the visitor name and passport number.
5. Pay with WeChat Pay or the supported method.
6. Save the confirmation and QR code.

Screenshot the ticket. The signal at the gate may be weak.

## Finding the right mini program

Search in Chinese. The English name may return travel articles or third-party sellers. Use the name from an official map listing or the attraction's website.

Look for the verified account badge and a booking flow that asks for passport details. Avoid paying a stranger through a personal QR code.

## Attractions that may need booking

- The Yangtze River Cableway.
- Major museums and memorial sites.
- Popular scenic areas during holidays.
- River cruises.
- High-speed rail tickets.

Rules change. Check the official page for the date you plan to visit.

## Passport details

Enter the passport number exactly, including letters and digits. Use the same name order as the passport. A mismatch can block entry.

For a group, add each visitor separately if the form requires it.

## If the mini program fails

- Ask the hotel front desk to help.
- Use the official ticket counter.
- Try the attraction's website.
- Book through a reputable platform with English support.
- Visit outside peak hours and buy on arrival.

## The backup plan

Save the Chinese name, address and opening hours of each attraction. Keep a screenshot of the booking and a photo of your passport details page. That covers the common failure points.

## The rule

Book only what needs a timed slot. For everything else, keep the day flexible and buy at the gate. Chongqing is easier when the schedule has room to move.
