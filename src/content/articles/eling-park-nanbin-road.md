## The short answer

Eling Park gives you a high, green view over the Jialing River and the stacked city. Nanbin Road gives you the river-level skyline. Together they make a good late-afternoon route with less crowd pressure than Hongyadong.

Start at Eling around 15:30, walk the ridge, then take a taxi across to Nanbin Road for sunset.

## Eling Park

Eling Park sits on a ridge in Yuzhong. It is a local park with trees, pavilions and viewing terraces. The main attraction is the panorama: the Jialing River bends below, bridges cross the water and the city rises in layers.

The park is not large, but the paths climb. Wear comfortable shoes and carry water in summer. The best views are from the ridge and the upper terraces.

## Nanbin Road

Nanbin Road follows the south bank of the Yangtze. The promenade is long, with restaurants, bars and open river views. It is one of the best places to see the peninsula skyline, especially after the office lights come on.

The road is not a single compact sight. Pick a section near the cableway or a known landmark, then walk along the water rather than trying to cover the whole length.

## The route

1. **15:30 — Eling Park.** Enter from the main gate, walk the ridge and find a view over the river.
2. **16:45 — Tea or a short rest.** The park has quiet corners and small places to sit.
3. **17:30 — Taxi to Nanbin Road.** Ask for a riverside section near the cableway or a major hotel.
4. **18:00 — Walk the promenade.** Find a spot before sunset and stay through blue hour.
5. **19:00 — Dinner.** Choose a restaurant set back from the water or continue to Jiefangbei.

## Photo notes

At Eling, use a telephoto to compress the river and the bridges. A wide lens shows the green foreground and the city beyond.

At Nanbin Road, a wide frame captures the skyline and the river. Use a railing or wall for stability at night. If the sky is hazy, shoot after the lights come on and let the city provide the contrast.

## What to bring

- Water and sun protection in summer.
- A light layer for the evening river breeze.
- A power bank for night photography.
- Cash or a payment app for small stalls.

## Is it worth it?

Yes, especially if you want a city view without the Hongyadong crowd. Eling is a local park, and Nanbin Road is a working riverfront. The combination feels more like daily Chongqing than a staged attraction.
