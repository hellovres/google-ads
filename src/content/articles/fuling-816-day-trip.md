## The short answer

Fuling 816 is a massive underground complex built into a mountain during the Cold War. It was intended to house a nuclear reactor and supporting facilities, but the project was halted before completion.

Visit on a guided tour. The scale is the attraction: cavernous tunnels, concrete halls and a sense of secrecy that is hard to find elsewhere.

## Getting there

Take a high-speed train from Chongqing to Fuling North, about 40 minutes. From the station, take a taxi to the 816 site, roughly 30 minutes depending on traffic.

Confirm the tour schedule before departure. The site is outside the city center, and the visit is organized in timed groups.

## What you will see

- A long main tunnel carved through the mountain.
- Large concrete chambers intended for the reactor and support systems.
- Exhibits on the project's history and the workers who built it.
- Dimly lit passages and industrial details.

The tour is as much about atmosphere as information. The emptiness of the halls is part of the story.

## Practical notes

- Wear comfortable shoes.
- Bring a light layer. The underground spaces are cool.
- Check photography rules at the entrance.
- Follow the guide and do not wander into closed areas.
- Allow extra time for the taxi back to the station.

## Is it worth it?

Yes if you are interested in Cold War history, engineering or large-scale underground spaces. It is a niche attraction and a long way from the city's classic sights.

If you have only three days in Chongqing, prioritize the city. If you have five days or a repeat visit, Fuling 816 adds a different layer.

## Combine it with

Return to Chongqing in the afternoon and visit the Three Gorges Museum for context. The two sites together explain the scale of modern Chinese infrastructure and the politics behind it.
