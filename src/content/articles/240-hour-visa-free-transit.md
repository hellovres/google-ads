## The short answer

China's 240-hour visa-free transit allows eligible travelers to enter through selected ports without a visa, provided they are transiting to a third country or region and meet the document requirements. Chongqing is one of the participating entry points.

The policy is useful, but the details matter. Your nationality, arrival port, onward destination and permitted travel area all need to fit the current rules. Check the official National Immigration Administration information before booking.

## The basic conditions

A typical transit case looks like this:

1. You are a citizen of an eligible country.
2. You arrive at an eligible port, such as Chongqing Jiangbei International Airport.
3. You have a confirmed onward ticket to a third country or region, departing within 240 hours.
4. Your passport is valid for the required period.
5. You stay within the permitted area.

The onward ticket is the critical piece. A return to the country you came from may not qualify as transit. The route needs to continue to a third destination.

## What to show at immigration

- Passport.
- Completed arrival card, if required.
- Confirmed onward ticket.
- Hotel booking or address.
- A clear explanation of your route.

Keep the documents on your phone and printed. Immigration officers may ask for the booking reference or the exact departure time.

## The 240-hour clock

The period begins at the time of entry, not at the time of your flight departure. Count the days carefully and leave a buffer for flight delays or changes.

Do not use the full window for a tight connection. A missed onward flight can turn a simple transit into an overstay.

## Travel area

The visa-free transit is tied to a permitted area. In some cases, travelers can move between specified provinces or municipalities. In others, the stay is limited to the entry region.

Before you plan a Chongqing-to-Chengdu-to-Xi'an route, confirm that every stop is inside the permitted area for your entry point and nationality. The rules change, and a plan that worked for someone else may not work for you.

## Common mistakes

- **Booking a return ticket to the same country.** The route may not count as transit.
- **Arriving at a port that is not eligible.** Check the airport and terminal.
- **Planning beyond the permitted area.** A domestic flight can still break the rule.
- **Leaving the application to the immigration queue.** Have the documents ready.
- **Assuming the policy applies to every nationality.** Check the current list.

## If you are not eligible

Apply for a standard tourist visa before departure. A visa gives you more flexibility on route and duration, and it avoids the risk of being denied boarding or entry.

## At Chongqing airport

Follow the signs for immigration and transit. Use the dedicated counter if one is available. Keep your onward ticket and hotel address accessible. If an officer asks about your route, answer simply and show the booking.

## The practical checklist

- Confirm nationality eligibility.
- Confirm Chongqing is an eligible port for your route.
- Book an onward ticket to a third country or region.
- Confirm the permitted travel area.
- Save hotel details offline.
- Arrive with time for questions.
- Keep a paper copy of the key documents.

The 240-hour transit can be a smooth way to see Chongqing. The travelers who run into trouble are usually the ones who treated it as automatic.
