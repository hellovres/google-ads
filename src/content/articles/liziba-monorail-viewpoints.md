## The short answer

Liziba is the Chongqing image most travelers already know: a monorail sliding through a residential tower as if the building were part of the track. The stop is on Metro Line 2, and the best experience takes under an hour.

Go for the street-level viewing platform first. Then ride one stop in either direction if you want the view from inside the train. Sunset is dramatic but crowded. Morning is calmer and easier for photos.

## Why the building exists

The tower and station were designed together. The lower floors hold the metro station and commercial space, while the upper floors are apartments. The train passes through a dedicated opening, with sound insulation and structural separation built into the design.

It is a practical solution to a steep site, and it has become one of the city's defining sights.

## The three viewpoints

### 1. The street-level viewing platform

Exit the station and follow the signs to the viewing area below the track. This is the classic angle: the train appears from the building, crosses the frame and disappears into the next slope.

A wide-angle lens works well. If you are using a phone, switch to the main camera and keep the building edge in the frame. The train arrives every few minutes, so you can watch the composition before committing.

### 2. Inside the station

Tap into the metro and walk to the end of the platform. From here, you can see the train emerge from the building and the river valley beyond. The station is a working transport hub, so keep bags and tripods clear of the doors.

This angle is better for video than stills. Stand to the side, brace your phone against the platform edge and let the train pass through the frame.

### 3. The riverside path

Walk downhill from the station toward the Jialing River. The path gives you a wider view of the tower, the track and the water. It takes more effort to reach, but the result feels more like a city scene and less like a checklist photo.

## Timing

Trains run frequently throughout the day. Aim for 08:00–10:00 for softer light and smaller crowds, or 17:00–19:00 for the evening skyline. The viewing platform can become dense around sunset, especially on weekends.

Avoid the middle of a public holiday if photography is the priority. The platform is small, and the crowd moves slowly.

## How to get there

Take Metro Line 2 to Liziba. Follow the signs for the viewing platform rather than the shopping center exit. The station is built into the hillside, so the correct exit matters.

From Jiefangbei, the ride usually involves a transfer at Linjiangmen or a short taxi. From the airport, take Line 10 and transfer to Line 2.

## Etiquette

This is a residential building. Keep voices low, do not block entrances and do not fly drones over the track. The station is a transport service, so let commuters pass before setting up a shot.

## Combine it with

- **Eling Park:** a short ride or uphill walk for a city view.
- **Testbed 2:** a creative district in a former factory, useful for a slower afternoon.
- **Nanbin Road:** continue toward the river for the skyline at dusk.

## The best shot

Put the building on the left third of the frame and leave space for the train on the right. Use a fast shutter if you want a crisp train, or a slower shutter for motion blur. Include a little of the hillside to show how the track sits above the street.
