## The short answer

Five days gives you the central city, a day trip and enough slack to eat well. Use the first three days for the classic route, day four for Wulong or Dazu, and day five for a slower neighborhood and river evening.

Keep Jiefangbei as the base. Add a river-view night only if you want the experience, not because the route requires it.

## Day 1: Arrive and meet the river

Check in, eat a simple meal and take an evening walk to Hongyadong. Do not schedule a major sight after a long flight. If you arrive early, add Liziba in the late afternoon.

## Day 2: Jialing side

Start at Liziba, then Eling Park. Have lunch near Jiefangbei. In the afternoon, walk Shibati or Kuixinglou. Finish with hotpot.

## Day 3: Yangtze side

Ride the cableway early. Walk Nanbin Road and Longmenhao. Return to the peninsula for a tea break, then visit Ciqikou in the late afternoon if you have energy.

## Day 4: Day trip

### Wulong

Take an early high-speed train. Visit the karst scenery and return in the evening. Book the train and the local transfer in advance.

### Dazu

Take a morning train to Dazu South, then a taxi or bus to Baodingshan. Spend two to three hours with the carvings and return by late afternoon.

The Wulong day is more physically demanding. Dazu is easier to combine with a slow evening.

## Day 5: Slow city and river

Visit the Three Gorges Museum or a smaller neighborhood. Have a long lunch, then take a river cruise or walk the promenade at dusk. Finish with a final bowl of noodles or a dessert.

## Where to stay

Stay in Jiefangbei for the whole trip. If you want a view, book one night in Nan'an on day three and return to the core for the day trip. Moving hotels twice in five days adds more friction than it removes.

## Budget

Five days in Chongqing can fit a wide range. A comfortable mid-range trip costs roughly CNY 2,500–4,500 per person excluding flights, depending on hotel choice, hotpot frequency and the day trip.

## What to book

- High-speed rail for the day trip.
- Hotel for five nights.
- Cableway ticket if you want a specific time.
- A river cruise if it is part of the plan.
- A hotpot reservation for the weekend.

## The pace

Five days should feel slower than three. Leave one afternoon without a plan, take a tea break and let the city's hills set the rhythm.
