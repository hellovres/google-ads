## The short answer

Ride the Yangtze River Cableway early. Buy a one-way ticket from Yuzhong to Nan'an, stand at the front window and continue on foot along the south bank. Return by metro or taxi instead of joining the queue again.

The ride is short. The value is the view of the river, the bridges and the layered city on both banks.

## Why the queue matters

The cableway is a working piece of transport history and a major photo stop. Capacity is limited, and the queue can swell quickly after 10:00. On weekends and holidays, the wait can exceed an hour.

The first hour after opening is the most reliable window. Late evening is the second-best option, especially if you want the city lights.

## Tickets and direction

Check the current price and operating hours at the station or in the official ticketing channel. Prices are modest, but the ticket type matters. A single journey is usually enough.

The classic direction is from the Yuzhong side, near Jiefangbei, to Nan'an. This gives you the skyline behind you and the south bank ahead. The reverse direction also works, but the approach and exit are less convenient for a first-time route.

If you buy a round trip, you may still face a queue on the return. A one-way ticket plus a metro or taxi back is often faster.

## Where to stand

Inside the cabin, move to the front or side window. The front gives you the advancing view of the far station and the river. The side gives you a wider angle across the bridges.

Keep your phone close and do not lean out. The cabin moves quickly, and the windows are shared.

## The south bank route

After landing in Nan'an, walk downhill toward the river. The promenade gives you a wide view back toward the peninsula. Continue toward Nanbin Road for lunch or coffee.

The area is spread out. Use a taxi for the return if the heat or hills are wearing you down.

## Timing the light

Morning is clean and cool. Sunset is dramatic but busy. If you want the night skyline, ride after dark and photograph from the south bank rather than trying to shoot through the cabin glass.

## Combine it with

- **Hongyadong:** a short ride or walk from the Yuzhong station.
- **Nanbin Road:** the riverfront walk on the far side.
- **Jiefangbei:** return for dinner and shopping.
- **Liziba:** pair it with a Line 2 ride later in the day.

## The realistic plan

Arrive 15 minutes before opening, buy a one-way ticket, ride across, walk the south bank for 30–40 minutes and return by taxi or metro. That sequence gives you the view without spending the best part of the morning in a queue.
