## The short answer

**Ciqikou** is the bigger, more famous old-town experience. It has the tiled roofs, snack stalls and river setting, but the main street is heavily commercial. Go before 10:00 and spend your time in the side lanes.

**Longmenhao** is smaller, quieter and more residential. It is less of a destination and more of a neighborhood walk. Choose it if you want atmosphere without the tour-group flow.

## Ciqikou

Ciqikou sits on the west bank of the Jialing River. The historic core has narrow lanes, stone steps, teahouses and shops selling snacks and crafts. The setting is genuinely attractive, especially in the early morning when the shutters are still coming up.

The main street is the busiest part. Walk uphill into the side lanes, where the crowd thins and the buildings show more of their age. The temple and the river viewpoints are worth the extra climb.

**Best for:** first-time visitors who want the classic old-town image, snack shopping and easy metro access.

**Watch out for:** souvenir stalls, loudspeakers and tour groups after 10:00.

## Longmenhao

Longmenhao is a hillside neighborhood in Nan'an with old houses, steep stairs and views toward the river. It has been partly restored, but it retains a lived-in feel. There are small shops, courtyards and places to sit.

The area is less organized for visitors. That is part of the appeal. Wear shoes with grip and be prepared to backtrack when a staircase ends in a courtyard.

**Best for:** repeat visitors, photographers and anyone who wants a slower old-street walk.

**Watch out for:** uneven steps, limited English signage and fewer obvious places to eat.

## The comparison

| Feature | Ciqikou | Longmenhao |
|---|---|---|
| Scale | Large | Small |
| Crowd | Heavy | Light to moderate |

| Access | Metro Line 1 | Bus or taxi |
| Best time | Early morning | Morning or late afternoon |
| Food | Snack-heavy | Small local spots |

## A route that includes both

Start at Ciqikou around 08:30. Walk the main street once, then turn uphill into the side lanes. Have a snack and leave before the midday crowd.

In the afternoon, take a taxi to Longmenhao. Walk the stairs slowly, stop for tea or a drink and continue toward the river. Finish with dinner in Nan'an or cross back to Jiefangbei.

## Which one should you choose?

Choose Ciqikou if this is your first trip and you want the old-town experience without a long detour. Choose Longmenhao if you have already seen the main sights and want a quieter, more textured walk.

If you have time for both, do Ciqikou early and Longmenhao late. The contrast makes each place easier to appreciate.
