## The short answer

Start at Liziba at 08:00, move to Jiefangbei for lunch, ride the cableway in the early afternoon and finish at Hongyadong after dark. Use taxis for the vertical connections and keep the plan flexible.

This route works for a layover, a single free day or an arrival day before a longer trip.

## The route

### 08:00 — Liziba

Take Metro Line 2 to Liziba. Watch the train enter the building from the street-level platform. Stay for two or three trains, then walk downhill for the wider view.

### 09:30 — Breakfast

Find a xiaomian shop near the station or ride to Jiefangbei. A bowl of noodles and soy milk takes 20 minutes.

### 10:30 — Jiefangbei and the pedestrian core

Walk the main square and the surrounding lanes. This is a good time for a first look at the city, a coffee and a quick shop.

### 12:00 — Lunch

Choose a local restaurant one block off the main street. Order a cold dish, a vegetable and a meat dish. Keep it light before the afternoon.

### 13:30 — Yangtze River Cableway

Take a taxi to the Yuzhong station. Buy a one-way ticket to Nan'an and ride across. Walk down to the riverfront and take a few photos of the skyline.

### 15:00 — Nanbin Road

Walk a short section of the promenade or find a cafe. Do not try to cover the whole road.

### 16:30 — Return to Yuzhong

Take a taxi or metro back. Rest for an hour if the heat has been strong.

### 18:00 — Early dinner

Eat near Jiefangbei or choose a hotpot restaurant. If you want the night view, keep dinner to 90 minutes.

### 19:30 — Hongyadong

Walk to Qiansimen Bridge for the classic view. Stay for blue hour, then walk back through Jiefangbei.

## Transport plan

- Metro for Liziba and the airport connection.
- Taxi for the cableway and the return from Nan'an.
- Walking for Jiefangbei and Hongyadong.

Avoid trying to walk between the cableway and Hongyadong during rush hour. The distance looks short, but the route includes stairs and crowded sidewalks.

## What to carry

- A charged phone with a payment app.
- A power bank.
- Water.
- A light rain jacket.
- A small bag for layers.

## If you have only half a day

Choose either Liziba plus Hongyadong, or the cableway plus Nanbin Road. The first gives you the icons. The second gives you the river.

## The one-day rule

Pick three anchors and let the rest be flexible. A rushed checklist in Chongqing usually means more stairs and less city.
