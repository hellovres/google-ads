## The short answer

Chongqing street food is best after dark, around metro exits, residential markets and the lanes behind commercial streets. Start with grilled skewers, fried potatoes and tofu. Pay by QR code and carry a little cash as backup.

The city does not have one official night market. It has many small food clusters, each with its own rhythm.

## Where to look

### Jiefangbei and the surrounding lanes

The main pedestrian area is convenient but expensive. Walk one or two blocks away for smaller shops and street stalls. Look for a cluster of stools, a bright grill and a short menu.

### Guanyinqiao

A younger, busier night scene with plenty of skewers, noodles and dessert shops. It is easy to combine with a hotel stay in the area.

### Shapingba

A student district with cheap, fast food and a high turnover of small shops. Good for an evening when you want variety without polish.

### Nan'an riverside

More restaurant-focused than stall-focused, but useful for a sit-down meal with a view. Street food is scattered along the side streets.

## What to try

- **Grilled skewers:** meat, tofu, vegetables and squid, cooked over charcoal.
- **Fried potato:** cubed or spiral-cut, tossed with chili and herbs.
- **Stinky tofu:** crispy outside, soft inside, with a strong fermented flavor.
- **Chongqing noodles:** a late-night bowl of xiaomian.
- **Tangyuan:** sweet rice balls in a warm syrup.
- **Bingfen:** a cold jelly dessert that helps with the heat.
- **Grilled corn:** coated in butter, chili or a savory sauce.

## How to order

Point at what you want and hold up the number of portions. Ask **duō shao qián** for the price. If you want it mild, say **wēi là**. If you want no chili, say **bú yào là**.

For skewers, the stall may charge by stick. Keep your sticks and pay at the end.

## How to pay

Most stalls display a QR code for Alipay or WeChat Pay. Scan it, enter the amount and show the confirmation. If the QR code is personal, the payment may go directly to the vendor.

Keep a few small notes for older stalls or a phone battery failure.

## How to eat safely

- Choose stalls with a queue and food cooked to order.
- Avoid raw or room-temperature items in hot weather.
- Check that meat is cooked through.
- Use the provided serving tools.
- Carry hand sanitizer.
- Drink bottled or filtered water.

## The route

Start at a metro exit with a cluster of stalls. Walk one block away from the main road, choose two or three items and eat standing up. Then find a dessert or a bowl of noodles and finish with a walk along the river.

That is the Chongqing night market experience: small portions, fast decisions and a lot of noise.
