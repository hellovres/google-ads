## Findings

- The closest dedicated competitor, `chongqingtravels.com`, ranks in the top six for the head term with only 39 posts. The niche is not saturated.
- Its articles run about 2,650-2,700 words. The strongest personal guide runs about 4,200 words. Depth matters, but 2,500-3,000 words is enough to compete.
- Head terms are held by authority sites. Long-tail queries are open: Liziba viewpoints, hotpot ordering, Wulong by train, 240-hour transit, where to stay.
- Monetization patterns are clear: AdSense plus Agoda, Klook and Trip.com. One competitor runs a multilingual tour-service funnel instead of ads.
- No competitor combines travel logistics with practical Mandarin. That is the clearest content gap.

## Content gaps to attack

- 2026 practicalities: 240-hour visa-free transit, foreign cards on Alipay and WeChat Pay, Didi in English, metro QR codes, WeChat mini-program tickets.
- Micro-locations: three Liziba viewing angles and timing, Kuixinglou, Hongyadong crowd windows, Nanbin Road, Eling Park, Shibati, Longmenhao, Huangjueping, Testbed 2.
- Day trips: Wulong by high-speed rail versus tour, Dazu, Fuling 816, Three Gorges cruise ship selection, Youyang.
- Food: hotpot ordering sheet, spice levels, xiaomian, jianghu cuisine, breakfast, night markets.
- Season and crowds: summer heat, fog season, public holidays, weekday versus weekend.
- Itineraries and budgets: 1, 2, 3 and 5 days, traveling with parents, traveling with kids, rainy-day backups.
- Decision content: Chongqing versus Chengdu, is Chongqing worth visiting, how many days.
- Transport details: Jiangbei Airport T3, Chongqing North and West stations, metro lines 2 and 3, cableway queue strategy.

## Strategy

- Do not fight the head term first. Win 30-40 long-tail queries, then build a pillar page that links them together.
- Publish 2,000-3,000 word guides with original photos, dated updates and first-hand detail.
- Refresh the top ten pages every quarter so freshness beats older authority pages.
- Add short video from Liziba, Hongyadong and Kuixinglou to increase dwell time and social reach.
- Earn links from Reddit r/travelchina, TripAdvisor forums, Quora, Pinterest and China travel blogs.
- Replicate the `mytravelbuzzg` monetization stack: AdSense plus Agoda, Klook and Trip.com, then add a paid itinerary.

## Realistic target

- 40-60 deep articles and 6-9 months to reach 10,000-20,000 sessions per month.
- At USD 10-25 RPM, AdSense alone is roughly USD 150-400 per month at that traffic level.
- With affiliates and a digital product, USD 300-800 per month is a reasonable month-6 to month-9 range.
